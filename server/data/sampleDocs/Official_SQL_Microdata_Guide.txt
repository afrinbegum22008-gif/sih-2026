TECHNICAL MANUAL: RELATIONAL QUERYING & SQL FOR OFFICIAL SURVEY MICRODATA

1. Overview and Relational Schema Design
Large-scale socio-economic surveys conducted by the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO), such as the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) and Consumer Expenditure Survey (CES), structure microdata across normalized tabular blocks:
- Block 1 & 2: Identification of Sample Household (State, District, Sub-sample, FSU, SSU, Sector).
- Block 3: Household Characteristics (Household Size, Religion, Social Group, Monthly Consumption).
- Block 4: Demographic and Activity Roster of Household Members (Person Serial No, Age, Gender, General Education, Usual Principal Activity, Current Weekly Status).

2. Critical SQL Patterns in Microdata Extraction
When conducting cross-sectional analysis, an analyst must link household level characteristics to individual person records.
A common failure in statistical compilation is the omission of households where some members were absent or non-respondents.
Therefore, standard INNER JOIN operations risk dropping valid sample units and distorting population weight sums.
The established best practice in official data curation is to utilize LEFT OUTER JOIN from the Master Household Block to the Person Block:

SELECT 
    h.fsu_no,
    h.sample_hh_no,
    h.state_code,
    h.sector,
    h.multiplier,
    p.person_sno,
    p.age,
    p.usual_principal_status
FROM plfs_hh_master h
LEFT OUTER JOIN plfs_person_roster p
    ON h.fsu_no = p.fsu_no AND h.sample_hh_no = p.sample_hh_no
WHERE h.sector = '1'; -- Rural Sector

3. Aggregation and Weighted Multipliers
In official statistics, unweighted row counts do NOT represent population counts. Every survey record carries an inflation factor or sampling multiplier (often divided by 100 or 200 depending on sub-sample pooling).
To estimate the total rural unemployed population:
SELECT 
    h.state_code,
    SUM(CASE WHEN p.usual_principal_status = '81' THEN h.multiplier / 100.0 ELSE 0 END) AS estimated_unemployed_persons
FROM plfs_hh_master h
JOIN plfs_person_roster p ON h.fsu_no = p.fsu_no AND h.sample_hh_no = p.sample_hh_no
GROUP BY h.state_code;

4. Analytic Partitioning with Window Functions
When benchmarking enterprise productivity in the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI), officers must rank factories within 2-digit National Industrial Classification (NIC) categories without collapsing the microdata records.
SQL Window Functions allow partition ranking:
SELECT 
    factory_id,
    nic_2digit,
    gross_output,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY nic_2digit ORDER BY gross_output DESC) as output_rank
FROM asi_factory_returns;

5. Summary of Competency Requirements
Officers operating in data management cells are expected to master:
- Relational schema modeling and outer join preservation.
- Weighted multiplier aggregation in SQL expressions.
- Window functions (ROW_NUMBER, RANK, DENSE_RANK, LAG, LEAD) for time-series and cross-sectional indices.
- Database indexing on compound survey keys (State + District + FSU + SSU).
